# Root faction head icons

The 13 faction head portraits in this directory are mirrored from the
**batmaster faction-heads** asset pack, hosted on Google Drive and
catalogued under each faction's `assets.headIcon[0]` block in
`src/state/definitions/root/modules.json`.

These are fan-created derivative artworks; "Root" and the underlying
game design are © Leder Games.

The mirrored copies here are for local rendering in this personal
project and are not redistributed beyond the deployed app.
